Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to restore the fundamental human right to digital privacy. We envision an internet where conversations are fleeting, untracked, and entirely owned by the participants. Modern communication systems are built on surveillance. Drift exists to break that cycle by introducing absolute digital ephemerality.

The Architecture of Silence

Mainstream applications rely on central servers that act as middlemen. Drift eliminates the middleman. By utilizing a strict Peer-to-Peer architecture, a direct, encrypted tunnel is formed exclusively between your two devices.

Absolute Zero-Data Guarantee

Even the creator of this system cannot see what you type or send. Drift possesses zero databases. When you refresh the page or sever the connection, your data ceases to exist permanently.

Security Advisory

Drift is fully anonymous. You can never be absolutely certain of who is on the other end unless verified externally.

  • Beware of social engineering tactics.
  • Never transmit financial assets or credentials to unverified peers.
  • This protocol is engineered for privacy, not the facilitation of harm.

  • Signaling Phase: Server acts as a temporary switchboard.
