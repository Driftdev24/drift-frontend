Mission Parameters

Our mission is to restore the fundamental human right to digital privacy. We envision a network where conversations are fleeting, untracked, and entirely owned by the participants. Drift introduces absolute digital ephemerality.

The Architecture of Silence

Mainstream systems rely on central servers acting as middlemen. Drift eliminates them. Utilizing a strict Peer-to-Peer architecture, a direct, encrypted tunnel is formed exclusively between endpoints.

Absolute Zero-Data Guarantee

Even the system creator cannot intercept your data. Drift possesses zero databases. When the connection is severed, your transit data ceases to exist permanently.

Security Advisory

Drift is anonymous. You cannot verify peer identity within this system.

  • Beware of social engineering tactics.
  • Never transmit financial assets to unverified peers.
  • This protocol is for privacy, not the facilitation of harm.
