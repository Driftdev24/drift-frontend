The Drift Protocol

The Problem Drift Solves

Modern communication systems are built on a foundation of data retention. Even mainstream "end-to-end encrypted" messaging applications rely on central servers that hoard metadata. They track who you talk to, when you log in, your IP address, and your device information. If a server is compromised, or if a company changes its privacy policy, your communication history and social graph are exposed.

The internet has lost the concept of a truly private, fleeting conversation—the digital equivalent of a whispered conversation in an empty room. Drift was created to fix this flaw by eliminating the server from the conversation entirely.

Mission and Vision

Mission: To provide an uncompromising, zero-metadata communication environment where privacy is enforced by mathematics and architecture, rather than by trust in a corporation.

Vision: To normalize digital impermanence. Drift believes that not every word needs to be recorded, analyzed, or stored. The ultimate goal is to facilitate "conversations that drift away"—leaving absolutely no trace behind once the connection is severed.

How Drift Works (Technical Architecture)

Drift achieves total ephemerality through a strict Peer-to-Peer (P2P) architecture.

  • The Signaling Phase: When a user creates a room, the Drift server acts merely as a temporary switchboard. It generates a cryptographically secure Room ID and holds the hashed password in its volatile RAM.
  • The Handshake: When the second user joins, the server introduces the two devices. They exchange connection coordinates (ICE candidates) to locate each other across the internet.
  • Direct Encrypted Tunnel: Once the devices find each other, they establish a direct WebRTC RTCDataChannel. The server steps completely out of the way.
  • Zero Server Routing: Text messages, voice notes, and images (up to 50MB) are transmitted directly from one browser to the other. The server never sees, processes, or routes the actual conversation.
  • Absolute Erasure (The Purge): Because there is no database, the conversation only exists in the temporary memory (RAM) of the two users' browsers. If either user refreshes the page, loses internet, or clicks "Leave," the P2P tunnel instantly collapses. The cryptographic keys are destroyed, and the data vanishes permanently.

Official Security and Usage Disclaimer

Drift is a privacy-first communication tool engineered strictly to protect the fundamental human right to secure, private conversation. It is built for individuals, professionals, and organizations who require absolute confidentiality and digital ephemerality to exchange sensitive ideas without the threat of data harvesting or metadata tracking.

Drift is not designed, intended, or endorsed for the facilitation of any illegal activities.

The architecture of this system—featuring zero data retention, peer-to-peer routing, and instantaneous memory purging—was developed to prevent corporate surveillance and protect personal privacy. Any misuse of this protocol to coordinate, discuss, or execute unlawful actions is a direct violation of the platform's core philosophy. Drift provides a secure space for legitimate, private communication; it does not harbor, monitor, or condone illicit behavior.
