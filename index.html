Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to restore the fundamental human right to digital privacy. We envision an internet where conversations are fleeting, untracked, and entirely owned by the participants. Modern communication systems are built on surveillance—they log your metadata, store your text, track your IP address, and commodify your data. Drift exists to break that cycle by introducing absolute digital ephemerality.

The Problem & Our Solution

Even so-called "End-to-End Encrypted" mainstream applications rely on central servers that act as middlemen. Drift eliminates the middleman. By utilizing a strict Peer-to-Peer (WebRTC) architecture, Drift only uses a server for the initial "handshake." Once you and your peer connect, a direct, encrypted tunnel is formed between your two devices.

Absolute Zero-Data Guarantee

Even the creator of this system cannot see what you type, send, or say. Drift possesses zero databases for message storage. We do not have the data, we do not want the data, and we lack the technical capability to view, intercept, or manipulate your conversations. When you refresh the page or close the tab, your data ceases to exist permanently.

An Independent Passion Project

Drift is not supported, funded, or endorsed by any corporation, government, or external entity. It is a standalone system built purely out of passion and the goal of creating a paradigm shift in how we handle online privacy.

ABOUT DRIFT

The Problem Drift Solves

Modern communication systems are built on a foundation of data retention. Even mainstream "end-to-end encrypted" messaging applications rely on central servers that hoard metadata. They track who you talk to, when you log in, your IP address, and your device information.

The internet has lost the concept of a truly private, fleeting conversation. Drift was created to fix this flaw by eliminating the server from the conversation entirely.

How Drift Works (Technical Architecture)

Drift achieves total ephemerality through a strict Peer-to-Peer (P2P) architecture.

  • The Signaling Phase: The server acts merely as a temporary switchboard to exchange connection coordinates.
  • Direct Encrypted Tunnel: Devices establish a direct WebRTC RTCDataChannel. The server steps completely out of the way.
  • Absolute Erasure: If either user refreshes the page, loses internet, or clicks "Leave," the P2P tunnel instantly collapses. The data vanishes permanently.

Official Security and Usage Disclaimer

Drift is a privacy-first communication tool engineered strictly to protect the fundamental human right to secure, private conversation. It is built for individuals, professionals, and organizations who require absolute confidentiality.

Drift is not designed, intended, or endorsed for the facilitation of any illegal activities.
